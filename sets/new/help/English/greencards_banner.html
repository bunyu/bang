Green Cards are semi-permanent cards. They are played on the table in front of the player, like the blue cards. But one cannot use the green cards on the same turn in which they were put on the table. After the end of the turn, green cards can be played like brown cards (out of turn, too). Green Cards can be stolen or discarded by other players using cards like "Panic!" and "Cat Balou". Note: you can't have two cards with the same name in front of you, but you can have two cards with the same effect.
