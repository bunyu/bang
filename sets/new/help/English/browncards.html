BANG!

BANG! cards are the main method to shoot a and reduce other players' life-points. You can only play BANG! cards on players within your shooting range! Max one BANG! per turn is normally allowed.

Missed!

If you are hit by a you may immediately play a Missed! - even though it is not your turn! - to cancel [] the shot. If you do not, you lose one life-point. If you lose your last life point, you are out of the game, unless you have a Beer to use "in extremis".

Beer

Play it in front of you to regain one life point. BEER is also used "in extremis" in case you're losing your last life-point. BEER has no effect when there are only 2 players left.

Panic!

"Draw a card" (steal) from "a player at distance 1". Remember that this distance is not modified by weapons, but only by cards such as Mustang and/or Scope.

Cat Balou

Force "any one player" to "discard a card", regardless of the distance.

Duel

Challenge any other player, regardless of the distance. The challenged player may play a BANG! card, then you may play a BANG! card, and so on: the first player failing to discard a BANG! card loses one life-point, and the duel is over.

Indians!

Each player, excluding the one who played this card, may respond with a BANG! card, or lose one life-point.

General store

When you play this card, every player gets a card! Starting with you and proceeding clockwise, each player chooses one of those cards and puts it in his hand.

Stagecoach

Draw two cards from the top of the deck.

Wells Fargo

Draw three cards from the top of the deck.

Gatling

The Gatling shoots a to "all the other players", regardless of the distance. The Gatling is not considered a BANG! card. During your turn you can play any number of Gatling, but only one BANG! card.

Saloon

This card makes all players, you included, regain one life-point (if possible). You cannot play a Saloon "in extremis": the Saloon is not a Beer!

Whisky (DODGE CITY)

You must discard an additional card to use this one. Play this card in front of you to regain 2 life-points.

Tequila (DODGE CITY)

You must discard an additional card to use this one. Play this card in front of you, or any other player to get or give 1 life-point.

Punch (DODGE CITY)

Same effect of a BANG! [], but only at a distance of 1; PUNCH does not count as a BANG!.

Springfield (DODGE CITY)

You must discard an additional card to use this one. Play this card on any player to shoot a . SPRINGFIELD does not count as a BANG!.

DODGE (DODGE CITY)

Same as MISSED! [], but also lets you draw an extra card [].

Rag Time (DODGE CITY)

You must discard an additional card to use this one. Play this card on any player to steal a card . In "normal" mode, you can also play this card on yourself by playing it to the center of the table.

Brawl (DODGE CITY)

You must discard an additional card to use this one. Play this card in front of you to make all other players discard a card.
