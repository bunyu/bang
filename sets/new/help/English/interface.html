 At all times, the cards you can play will be bright, while the cards you can't play will be dimmed out.
You can only play one BANG! per turn, unless your weapon is a Volcanic. All blue cards are played on the table in front of you, except Jail which is played on any other player.

Detailed information on any selected card is displayed in the help bar at the top of the screen.

Distance and Range

Players at your sides are both considered to be at a distance of 1. Then the next player (left or right) are at a distance of 2 and so on.
Distance can be modified by blue cards in play. Your base range is 1, so you can't shoot at a distance of 2 or more in the beginning, but many weapons extend your shooting range to 2 or more.
Cards with the symbol are defensive: they add 1 to the normal distance of that player.
Cards in play with the symbol are attack cards: if you have such card in play, you will "see" other players at a distance decreased by 1.
The purposes of and are symmetrical and you normally wish to have both: the former for defense from near players, the latter to attack far players.
and affect all cards that specify a range.
Confused? Don't worry!
All distance modifiers are applied automatically, so you don't have to worry about it: just bear in mind that you want to have both and you don't want other players to have them!
