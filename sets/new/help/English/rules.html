BANG!

BANG! is a shootout game, in Spaghetti-Western style, between a group of Outlaws and the Sheriff, who is their primary target. The Deputies incognitos help the Sheriff, but there is also a Renegade pursuing his own goal!
In BANG! each player plays one of these roles, and represents a famous Wild-West inspired character.

OBJECT OF THE GAME

Each player has a goal that depends on the role:
Sheriff:must eliminate all the Outlaws and the Renegade, to protect law and order.
Outlaws:they would like to kill the Sheriff, but they have no scruples about eliminating each other to gain rewards!
Renegade:he wants to be the new Sheriff; his goal is to be the last character in play.
Deputies:they help and protect the Sheriff, and share his same goal, at all costs!
3-player match
In a 3-player match, all roles are public and the objectives are as follows:
Deputy:must eliminate the Renegade!
Renegade:must eliminate the Outlaw!
Outlaws:must eliminate the Deputy!
In case another player kills the designed target, the goal becomes to remain the last one standing.

Characters

Each Western character has some special abilities, making that character unique. Characters with very powerful abilities have one life-point less. Moreover, the number of life-points indicate how many cards the player can hold in his hand at the end of his turn (hand size limit). Character abilities are disable in "easy" game mode.

THE GAME

The Sheriff begins. The game is played in turns, in clockwise order. Each player's turn is divided into three phases:
  1. Draw cards;
  2. Play any number of cards;
  3. Discard excess cards.

Draw cards

Draw the top two cards from the draw pile, unless modified by special abilities or events: e.g. Dynamite and Jail chance cards are checked first.

Play any number of cards

Now you may play cards to help yourself or hurt the other players, trying to eliminate them.
You are not forced to play cards during this phase. You can play any number of cards; there are only three limitations:
  • you can play only one "BANG!" card per turn (unless you have a Volcanic gun or a special ability); this applies only to "BANG!" cards, not to all other cards with the symbol.
  • you can have only 1 copy of any one card in play on the table in front of you; (e.g. you can't have two Barrel cards, two Mustang cards, etc.).
  • you can have only 1 weapon in play.
There are two types of cards: brown cards (= instant cards) and blue cards (= permanent cards, like weapons, horses and other modifiers).
Some cards are targeted to a specific other character, play them onto the corresponding player.
Some other cards affect all players or just yourself, play them on the center of the table.
Brown cards are instant cards: they will apply their effect and go on the discard pile.
Blue cards are permanent: they stay "in play" on the table in front of the corresponding player.
Green Cards (available only with the optional "Dodge City" cards expansion pack) are semi-permanent: they are played on the table first and then (after the end of the turn) they can be played from the table and have an instant effect, like Brown cards.
All cards, including Blue Cards, can be removed or stolen as a result of other cards like "Panic!" and "Cat Balou".

Discard excess cards

Before passing the turn to the next player, you must discard from your hand any cards exceeding the number of your life-points.

Distance between players

The distance between two players is the minimum number of places between them, counting clockwise or counterclockwise. The distance is very important, because all cards with a range take it into account. Initially, you can reach only targets (players or cards) within a distance of 1. When a character is eliminated, he is no longer counted when evaluating the distance: some players will get closer when someone is eliminated.

Eliminating a character

When you lose your last life-point, you are eliminated and your game is over, unless you immediately play a Beer "in extremis" (see below). When you are eliminated from the game, your role becomes public and all your cards are discarded.

Penalties and Rewards

  • If the Sheriff eliminates a Deputy, the Sheriff must discard all the cards he has in hand and in play.
  • Any player eliminating an Outlaw (even if the eliminating player is himself an Outlaw!) gets a reward of 3 cards from the deck.

END OF THE GAME

The game ends when one of the following conditions is met:
a) the Sheriff is killed. If the Renegade is the only one alive, then he wins. Otherwise, the Outlaws win;
b) all the Outlaws and the Renegade are killed. The Sheriff and his Deputies win.
Example 1. All the Outlaws are eliminated, but the Renegade is still in play. In this case, the game continues. The Renegade must now face the Sheriff and his Deputies alone.
Example 2. The Sheriff is killed, but all Outlaws are eliminated, and one Deputy and the Renegade are still in play. The game ends with the Outlaws winning! They achieved their goal
at the cost of their own lives!

THE CARDS

Weapons

You start the game with a Colt .45 revolver. This is not represented by any card and is implicit.
Using the Colt .45 you can only hit targets at a distance of 1, i.e. only players to your right or your left.
In order to hit targets farther than distance 1, you need to play a bigger weapon. Weapons can be recognized from their blue border, black-and-white illustration and the number into the sight that represents the maximum reachable distance.
The weapon in play substitutes the Colt .45, until the card is removed somehow.
Even if weapons are played on the board, they can still be stolen (e.g. through the play of a Panic!) or discarded (e.g. through Cat Balou).
The only weapon you can never lose is the ol' Colt .45!
You can only have one weapon in play at a time: if you play a new weapon when you already have one, the previous one is discarded.
Important: weapons do not change the distance between players. They represent your maximum reachable distance when shooting.
Volcanic: with this card in play you may play any number of BANG! cards during your turn. These BANG! cards can be aimed at the same or different targets, but are limited to a distance of 1.

BANG! and Missed!

BANG! cards are the main method to reduce other players' life-points. You can play a BANG! card to hit one of the players, only if the distance to that player is less than or equal to your weapon's shooting range.
If you are hit by a BANG! you may immediately play a Missed! - even though it is not your turn! - to cancel the shot.
If you do not, you lose one life-point. If you have no more life-points, you are out of the game, unless you play immediately a Beer "in extremis" (see next paragraph). You can only cancel shots aimed at you.

Beer

This card lets you regain one life-point.
The Beer cannot be used to help other players, but other cards with the same effect exist.
The Beer can be played in two ways:
  • usual, during your turn;
  • off-turn, "in extremis", only if you have just received a hit that is lethal (i.e. a hit that takes away your last life point)
Beer has no effect if there are only 2 players left in the game.
Example. You have 2 life points left, and suffer 3 damages from a Dynamite.
If you play 2 Beers you will stay alive with 1 life point left (2 - 3 + 2), while you would be eliminated playing only one Beer that would allow you to regain just 1 life point... you would still be at zero!

Saloon

Saloon has the same effect of the beer, but applies to all players.
The overall effect is that all players in play regain one life-point.
You cannot play a Saloon out of turn "in extremis": the Saloon is not a Beer!

THE SYMBOLS ON THE CARDS

Each card shows one or more symbols that show the effect(s) of the card.
  1. Main Effect
  2. Card Target
  3. Side Effect
  4. Card Tag

Main Effect

The first symbol on the card is the effect to apply. The most common effects are:
The effect of a "BANG!" card: attempt to remove one life-point from the specified target.
The effect of "Missed!" card: cancel the effect of
Regain one life-point. Applies to the player playing this card or to the specified target.



Draw one, two or three cards. If "any player" is specified (see target symbol), then you can draw a random card from his hand, or you can choose one in play in front of him. If no player is specified, draw the top card of the deck. In any case, cards drawn are added to your hand.
Force to discard a card. You can force a specified player to discard a random card from his hand, or you can choose and discard one card in play in front of him.

Card Target

Some cards have a specific target, otherwise they apply to the character who played them (e.g. "Beer") or have special rules (e.g. "Indians!"):

Side Effect

Very few cards also have a Side Effect. Side Effects are additional effects that are applied when you play that card, usually, they are:
When you play this card, you have to discard another card as well (this is usually because the card you are playing is powerful).
Draw an additional card as you play this one!

Card Tag

In the lower right corner of the card there's an additional symbol. You don't need it normally, it is used when you draw a card for a chance of something special happening: some cards, like Dynamite and Jail, require you to draw for your chance at the beginning of your turn, some other, like Barrel do the same while other players attack you, for an additional defense.
Only two tags are needed at present:
This means you're lucky! The Barrel will protect you from a shot, the Jail will let you escape, etc.
This is only used for the Dynamite: when you draw for your chance and you see this tag, the Dynamite will explode and you'll lose 3 life-points! Otherwise, nothing happens and the Dynamite goes to the next player.
You don't have to worry about the other tags in the base game: they are reserved for expansions.

REMEMBER:

  • Any card with a symbol can be used to cancel the effect of a card with a symbol
  • you can only play one "BANG!" card per turn, but you can play any number of other cards which show the symbol
  • you cannot have two cards with the same name in front of you
  • you can only have one weapon in play, but at least you always have an "implicit" Colt .45 that can't be stolen nor removed
  • weapons do not change the distance between players, they represent only your shooting range
  • Beer has no effect when there are only 2 players left in the game
  • when you are losing your last life point, you can only play a Beer "in extremis" and not a Saloon.
