Bill Noface

During phase 1 of his turn, he draws 1 card, plus 1 card for each injury (lost life-point) he currently suffers.

Calamity Janet

She can use BANG! [] cards as MISSED! [] cards and vice versa. The usual "one BANG! per turn" limitation still applies.

Claus the Saint

He draws one card more than the number of players, keeps 2, then gives 1 to each other player.

Achievement: Oh-oh-ooh!

Elena Fuente

She can use any card in her hand as a MISSED! [].

Greg Digger (SCAVENGER PACK)

Each time a character is eliminated from the game, Greg gains 2 life points.

Achievement: One foot in the grave

Herb Hunter (SCAVENGER PACK)

Each time a character is eliminated from the game, Herb draws 2 extra cards.

Achievement: Zombie Revenge

Paul Regret

All players add 1 to the distance to him. If he has a MUSTANG in play, it works normally, increasing all distances to him by a total of 2.

Pedro Ramirez

During phase 1 of his turn, he may choose to draw the first card from the top of the discard pile or from the deck.

Pixie Pete

During phase 1 of his turn, he draws 3 cards instead of 2.

Rose Doolan

She sees the other players at a distance decreased by 1. If she has a SCOPE in play, it works normally, reducing her distance to all other players by a total of 2.

Sean Mallory

At the end of his turn, he can hold up to 10 cards (instead of the number of life-points) in his hand.

Tequila Joe

Each time he plays a BEER, he regains 2 life-points instead of 1. He only regains 1 life-point from similar cards like SALOON.

Vulture Sam (SCAVENGER PACK)

Each time a character is eliminated from the game, Sam takes all of his cards and adds them to his own hand.

Achievement: Cain

Willy the Kid

He can play any number of BANG! [] cards during his turn.
