Sheriff

The Sheriff must eliminate all the outlaws and the renegade(s) to protect law and order. The Sheriff starts the game with an additional life-point and is always the first to play. The Sheriff can't be put in jail.

Deputy

The deputies help and protect the Sheriff, and share the same goals: at all costs! In fact, if the sheriff wins, all deputies win, even if they were eliminated.

Outlaw

Oulaws want to kill the Sheriff, but they have no scruples about eliminating each other to gain rewards! However, all outlaws (even those who were eliminated) win if the Sheriff is killed.

Renegade

The Renegade wants to be the new Sheriff: his goal is to be the last character in play. So, the Renegade must eliminate all other characters first, and finally the Sheriff.
